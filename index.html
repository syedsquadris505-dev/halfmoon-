How a batch gets to you

Every bag follows the same four stages, and every stage is logged.

Sourcing

Green coffee

Bought from estates we can name, sampled and cupped before we commit to a lot.

Roasting

Drum, 12 kg

Charged at 196 °C and dropped between 205 and 212 °C, depending on the coffee.

Profile saved per batch
Resting

Five days

Coffee degasses in valve bags until it tastes settled, then it is packed.

Shipping

Within 48 hours

Ground to your brewer on request and sent with the roast date on the bag.
